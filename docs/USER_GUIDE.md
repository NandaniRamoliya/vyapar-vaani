# User guide (for shopkeepers)

## Start

1. Open the app link (or the icon on your home screen) and log in with the ID and password your admin gave you.
2. Choose your language at the top: **EN · हिं · ગુ**. Every screen switches.
3. Tap the big **mic** and speak normally. After Vaani answers, the mic opens again by itself, so you can keep
   talking. When you go quiet it stops. (Turn this off with the ✋ button.)

> Speak in any of the three languages, even mixed in one sentence. If you speak a different language from the one
> selected, Vaani answers in yours and switches the app. Settings → *Reply in the language I speak*.

Vaani also understands:
- **Follow-ups:** "uska kitna baaki?" (about the last customer), "Priya ke liye bhi" (same again for Priya), "aur 500".
- **Missing details:** if you forget the amount or name, she asks. Just answer: "5000" or "Rahul".
- **Saving:** before saving, she reads it back. Say **"haan / yes / હા"** to save or **"nahi / no / ના"** to cancel.

## Customer voice signature (for udhar)

After an udhar entry, Vaani asks the customer to say, for example, *"Haan, maine 5000 udhar liye"*. Hold the phone
towards the customer; recording stops when they finish. The entry is marked **customer's voice**.
If they later deny it, say *"Rahul ka saboot sunao"* or open **Khata → Rahul** and press ▶. Use **Share** to send
the clip on WhatsApp.

## Screens

- **Today:** sold today, money received, udhar given, total udhar in market, today's tasks, who to collect from.
- **Khata:** everyone's balance; open a customer for their full history, add "Gave udhar" or "Got money", send a WhatsApp reminder.
- **Reminders:** when it's time, Vaani rings, speaks the reminder and shows a phone notification, with **Call**,
  **WhatsApp**, **Done** and **10 min later**. Tap **Allow alerts** once. Keep the app open (it can run in the background).
- **Bills, Customers, Conversation log, Settings:** under **More** on phones.

## Tips

- Use **Chrome on Android** for the best Hindi and Gujarati recognition.
- No internet? Type in the box (⌨️). Typing works like speaking, including typing "haan" to save.
- Install it: Chrome menu ⋮ → **Add to Home screen**.
